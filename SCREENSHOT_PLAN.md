## Presentation rules

- Crop browser chrome when possible.
- Do not show passwords, database credentials, private customer information, or session identifiers.
- Keep screenshots at a consistent aspect ratio.
- Use 4–6 strong screenshots rather than a large gallery.
- Lead with the homepage, then show features that demonstrate engineering depth.
