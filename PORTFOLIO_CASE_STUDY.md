## The Project

Silverlight is a fitness and wellness e-commerce platform that combines a customer-facing storefront with an administrator control center.

Users can browse, search, and sort products, view product details, manage their cart, place orders, leave comments, contact the business, and use a BMI calculator. Administrators can manage products, inventory, orders, gyms, trainers, comments, and customer messages.

The original application was built collaboratively from scratch. I spearheaded the group and took significant responsibility for the backend and database implementation. I later led the final completion and modernization phase, bringing the application from a functional development project to a polished, deployed application.

## Engineering Highlights

**Transaction-safe checkout**  
Checkout validates inventory and coordinates order creation, order items, inventory updates, user/order relationships, and cart clearing inside a database transaction.

**Inventory protection**  
The application validates available inventory during cart and checkout operations to reduce the risk of overselling.

**Role-based administration**  
Administrative routes and destructive actions are restricted to authorized users.

**Authentication and session handling**  
Login and registration include validation, session management, and session ID regeneration. Logout clears the session and uses no-cache headers to prevent stale authenticated pages from being displayed.

**Database-driven application**  
The application uses relational tables and PDO-based queries to support users, products, carts, orders, comments, replies, contact questions, gyms, and trainers.
